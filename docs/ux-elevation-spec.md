## 0. In ten lines

1. **Three tabs: Hoy · Ganancias · Comparar.** Cuenta sits behind the person icon. The reader is a *status*, not a tab; Fiscal is a monthly review, not a tab; Historial is the week stepper, not a settings row.
2. **One hero per screen, one primary action, at most one number per row in the first viewport.** Hoy: is the reader working and what do I have left today. Ganancias: my week/month as a timeline. Comparar: one metric at a time.
3. **The chip tells the truth.** The trip's colour band against the driver's two lines + a solid ▲ ▬ ▼ position cue + one deciding rate + "neto" + the other rate as small information. A reader failure is **gris, never red**. The deciding unit never swaps. No brand strip, no K, no verdict word. **Drag only:** tap and long-press do nothing over the host; thresholds are changed in the app (Tu semáforo, or "Ajustar mis límites" from an offer's detail). The overlay editor was removed because it could only ever open during a live offer.
4. **The signature is "la regla".** The driver's two limits are two labelled lines on the chip's ink plate — "tu meta $8" above, "tu mínimo $6" below — and the trip is a full-width colour band above both (verde), between them (amarillo) or below both (rojo), with a solid ▲ ▬ ▼ cue; gris has no band and dashed lines. Up means more money. The same object recurs at every scale: the mark that leads every offer row, the four marks that teach the states on the demo (A1), la regla at 1:1 over the two limit controls in Tu semáforo (G2), the car-cost line the week's bars split on in Ganancias, and the reference line with its caption hung under it in Comparar (and for the IMSS minimum on E5). No gauge, no traffic light, no verdict word, no coloured rounded card.
5. **The visual system is paper, ink and the raya.** One white day surface (`--paper` #FFFFFF) with ink text; Barlow for the UI and Barlow Semi Condensed for money; tab roots carry a 30sp title and a 72sp hero (the Round 14 system layer); no cards, no pills, no icon tiles, no brand strip; verdict colour appears only on verdicts (the chip and the mark) and once more as the green dot of "Lector funcionando"; errors and every other reader state are ink; the app follows the system theme and ships a night set; the chip is theme-independent.
6. **Money words mean one thing each.** "Te quedan / Te quedaron" with "aprox." in the label and one quiet line under the number — the count and the deduction basis, "6 viajes tomados, ya sin gasolina ni desgaste ›", which opens Cómo se calculó — on every period hero; "neto" only as the unit on rates; "Te pagó la app" instead of "bruto"; offers are "La tomaste · aprox." until a payment is confirmed; readiness is "preparado" vs "funcionando" (and "funcionando" expires).
7. **Onboarding = demo → one disclosure → Activar → (battery only if a restriction is detected) → one cost question → WhatsApp number, optional.** Value in 5 s, the permission while motivation is fresh, reader *preparado* in ~60–90 s, no account before value, no "ya está leyendo" before anything was read.
8. **Free feels complete:** reader, today, this week + 3 previous, current month, IMSS review (with the official verification route as its primary action), Tus apps, share, import. **Premium:** the breakdown of older weeks, city comparisons, the accountant's PDF. Locks mask the other side's number or the breakdown, never the driver's own total.
9. **Trial = 14 days, no card, no charge at the end, explicit start** when there is something paid to show; its terms are shown apart from any renewal language. Data eligibility is explained *before* purchase, never as a surprise after. Nobody is charged for absent data.
10. **Kill list:** Lector tab, Fiscal tab, Historial screen, Resumen de la semana, Simulador as a destination, the 3-page pitch and its "30 %", the Lector mock card, the 5-column table, "Dónde puedes ganar más", coaching consejos, streak, goal bar, "Cubierto este mes", the second consent dialog, the "$200" promise, sliders over a live offer, 55 dead strings.
11. **Kept and fixed:** import (with an honest "este archivo sí se sube" line), IMSS review (free, factual), referral (under Cuenta), share card (preview = PNG, amounts hidden by default).
12. **Scores:** today 27–31/80. The first v2 draft scored 59 (Opus) and 51 (Codex) in Round 3 — both failed it on the same defects: mockup arithmetic that didn't run through the formula, net-vs-gross comparisons, an undisclosed data flow, unvalidated paywall claims. Rounds 4–10 fixed every confirmed defect (§9b–§9e) and Round 10 passed both reviewers on the structural rubric (Opus 69, Codex 68). Round 11 then scored the same version **16/40 by both reviewers on the craft rubric** (§9g, §10): structure passed, feel did not. Round 12 scored the v3 rebuild: **Codex PASS (68/80 + 34/40); Opus NOT_YET (71/80 + 33/40)**, one point short on the signature ("it stops at Tu día"). Round 13 carried the signature into Ganancias, Comparar, Tu semáforo and the demo and fixed both lists (§9h); **both reviewers passed it on both rubrics on the same version — Codex 71/80 + 35/40, Opus 73/80 + 35/40** — and a residual pass applied their bounded lists (§9i), which both confirmed in Round 13b (Codex 71/80 + 35/40, Opus 74/80 + 36/40). The bar is a double pass (≥ 68/80 and ≥ 34/40, both reviewers, same version); glance physics (criterion 10) and time-to-value (criterion 7) stay at 8 for both reviewers until the mounted glance test (R14) and a timed install exist. **Round 14:** a blind template test (§9j) against 12 generic chips and 4 generic Hoy screens from fresh models chose the chip "la regla" (the best Uber-class rank, 0/6 template votes) and Hoy v5 (Uber-class 8.1, the best of 7); both are this version. The first-encounter glance still favours the template's verdict word and road-sign icons — the mounted test (R14) decides.

## 2. The elevated concept

**Promise (es-MX, in the driver's words):** «Antes de aceptar, veo si el viaje me deja de verdad. Al cerrar el día sé cuánto me quedó, y al cerrar la semana sé cómo voy.»
**In-product line:** **"Ve si el viaje te deja, antes de aceptarlo."**
**Promise (English, for us):** at the offer, one glance says whether the trip pays *after the driver's own costs*; afterwards the app shows what they kept and, when real data exists, how they compare. It never touches the ride app and screen content never leaves the phone.

### The driver's day

| Moment | Question | Kompara does | Surface |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before the shift | "¿Ya funciona?" | Shows real readiness: *preparado* (permission on, nothing read yet) vs *funcionando* (a real offer was read), per app. | Hoy status · Lector |
| **The offer (8–20 s)** — moment 1 | "¿Me conviene con mis costos?" | One stable rate + colour + shape in < 150 ms, or an honest "sin lectura". Never acts. | Chip |
| During the trip | — | **Nothing.** Silent while a passenger is in the car. | — |
| Between trips (parked) | "¿Cómo voy hoy?" | Today's result and the last offers, as proof the reader is reading. | Hoy |
| **End of the day** — moment 2 | "¿Cuánto me quedó?" | One quiet summary; the day, offer by offer. | Notification → Tu día |
| **Week close (Monday)** — moment 3 | "¿Fue buena semana? ¿Cómo voy contra los demás?" | The week as a timeline, one comparison, one share, the trial invitation. | Notification → Ganancias → Comparar → Compartir |
| Month | "¿Ya llegué al monto del IMSS? ¿Qué le paso a mi contador?" | A factual monthly review per app; a PDF for the accountant (Premium). | Ganancias › Mes → IMSS |

Build around **the offer, the day, the week.** Readiness is the prerequisite for all three.

### The signature

*Your two limits are two labelled lines on the plate; the trip's colour band sits above them, between them or below them.* "La regla" is the one visual idea that is Kompara's: the product's central fact (net after *your* costs against *your* meta and *your* mínimo) drawn as two lines the trip is positioned against. Other reader apps also judge an offer's net against the driver's own threshold; the distinction is the form, not the arithmetic. Up means more money. A solid ▲ ▬ ▼ cue repeats the position for a first encounter; no verdict word, no brand strip, no traffic light, no gauge, no coloured rounded card. The chip is the signature at full size (§6); the same object carries through the app at smaller scales:

- **Hoy:** reader status is words led by a mark — a green dot when it works ("Lector funcionando", the one non-verdict use of verde: "it works"), a 24 × 2dp ink segment otherwise (broken "━ ━" preparado · dashed apagado / en pausa); offer rows are led by the mark (§4), with the offer's meaning in the chip's own words under the outcome ("Arriba de tu meta").
- **Tu día:** three shift rows led by a solid (en turno) or dashed (pausa) segment; offer rows led by the mark. No invented offers (the fixture has four).
- **Ganancias · Semana:** the raya is **the car cost** — one 2dp ink line across the day bars at the daily share of lo del coche ($1,500 ÷ 7 = $214.29, 15.5 % of the $1,380 scale; 28 % of the $762 lunes bar on the open week), captioned at its right end between its two words, "Lo del coche" above and "$214 por día" below; every bar splits on it (`--rule` below, the car's share; `--ink-3` above, what you kept), so the hero is exactly the ink above the line (E1, E7b, E8, N4). **Mes** has no car line: its bars, already after the car cost, take the same light `--ink-3` fill at Semana's 30dp bar width on the 2dp $0 raya (E3, E3c, N5).
- **Comparar:** the reference is the 2dp line with its caption hung 4dp under it (`.ref`) — "Promedio · $131/viaje" on Tu ciudad, "Uber · tu app principal · $9.13/km" on Tus apps, "Tú · 5 – 11 oct · $107/viaje" on F4 — and the compared row ("Tú $149/viaje", "DiDi $10.34/km") stands above it led by the ink mark, because a comparison is not a verdict; a reference that does not exist yet is a dashed line (F5). The share card carries the same object without amounts — "Tú" with the ink mark above the 2dp line, "Promedio" hung under it (H3) — so the signature leaves the app. The IMSS minimum (E5) takes the car line's anatomy.
- **Tu semáforo:** la regla itself at 1:1, replaying an offer above the two limit controls, "Tu meta · verde desde" and "Tu mínimo · rojo debajo de" — the lines the driver edits are the chip's own lines, and the band moves as they change (G2).
- **Demo:** the four marks under the one-line legend are the state selector (A1), stepped through once on first view.
- **Brand:** the K square is gone. In product the K is an unboxed Barlow Semi Condensed 700 letter in verde (the demo's wordmark, the share card, the notification icon; never on the chip). The K-raya launcher mark (a K whose arms spring from a horizontal bar) is a founder fork (§9g).

### What we take from the Uber Driver app (structure, not brand)

- **Status-first home.** Uber's home answers "am I online?" before anything else. Hoy answers "¿está leyendo?" and, when the answer is no, turns into one ink cause and one big button — our "GO" (B2).
- **One hero number.** Today's result at 72sp, alone.
- **Earnings as a timeline.** Week ↔ month ↔ day ↔ trip, with a 7-bar chart and arrows; one screen serves every week.
- **Trip-card discipline.** Every offer: time · rate · verdict · what you did. Detail one tap deeper, in a sheet.
- **Settings tucked behind Account**, each row showing its current value.
- **One top-app-bar pattern** on every drill-in; full-screen tasks without the bottom bar.
- **Quiet notifications**, only for events the driver would want.

**What we do not copy:** a map (no location, no duplication of the ride app); a fake GO toggle (the real switch is Android Accessibility); an inbox/promotions feed; quests, streaks or "maneja más" pressure; any "Aceptar/Rechazar" control or label; Uber's palette or marks.

### What we cut

## 3. Information architecture

### Navigation

| # | Tab | Icon | One job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Hoy** | `Home` | Is Kompara reading, what do I have left today, what happened with my offers. |
| 2 | **Ganancias** | `bar_chart` (vector drawable) | My money over time: week ↔ month ↔ day ↔ offer. |
| 3 | **Comparar** | `compare_arrows` (vector drawable) | My apps against each other, or me against drivers of my city — one metric at a time. |
| — | **Cuenta** | a bare 24dp person icon, top-right of every tab root | Plan, semáforo, costs, reader, notifications, invites, help, data. |

**Why Comparar and not Cuenta as the third tab:** the founder's locked principle (Inicio = individual, Comparar = comparison), Comparar is the visible paid destination, and Codex's "Tus apps" segment makes it useful before any city cohort exists. Uber's Account slot is honoured by the always-visible person icon. (Fork F1; both reviewers ended here.)

**Chrome rules (v3 — paper, ink and the raya)**
- Tab roots: a 72dp top bar with the 30sp root title (money face 700) at the left ("Hoy", "Ganancias", "Comparar"; on Comparar the scope **text tabs** "Tus apps | Tu ciudad" are a 16sp tab row under the title, the Ganancias grammar, each tab reserving its 600 width so switching never shifts the row) and, at the right, at most one action icon plus the account control — a bare 24dp person icon (never a ring, never a circled glyph); the share icon sits beside it where a period can be shared. **No "K Kompara" wordmark bar** in the app proper: the K (an unboxed Barlow Semi Condensed 700 letter in verde) appears only on the demo's first screen, the share card and the notification icon.
- Share is **a share icon in the top bar** (24dp, `--ink-2`), never a button on the surface: on every Ganancias frame and on Comparar › Tu ciudad when a standing exists (F2, F3); absent on Hoy, Tus apps (F1), F4 and F5.
- Drill-ins: a 64dp top bar with ← (48dp target) and a 20sp title, at most one trailing control (IMSS del mes chooses its month with the Ganancias period stepper under the bar, `‹ Octubre · en curso ›`, not a top-bar dropdown; on Tu día the title "Tu día" stays and the day stepper sits as a cluster at the right of the same row). Full-screen tasks entered from a flow — Premium, Compartir, Importar's steps — use ✕ in the same slot; Tu plan (H2–H2d) and Corregir (H5d) keep ←. **Every H task names its flow as the 20sp top-bar title** — "Premium" (H1, H1b), "Tu plan" (H2, H2b, H2c, H2d), "Compartir" (H3), "Importar" (H4–H5e) — and the content opens with the screen's own hero or title; there are no 13sp flow labels.
- Selectors are **text tabs** (active `--ink` 600 with the 24 × 2dp underline, inactive `--ink-2`, unavailable `--ink-3`), the **period stepper** (a 48dp row, `‹ 12 – 18 oct · semana pasada ›`, also IMSS del mes's month selector, 48dp arrow targets, the forward arrow dimmed to `--ink-3` on the current period; the label opens Elegir periodo) and **context selectors** that open choice sheets (on Comparar the hero's 48dp context row, a 16sp `--ink-2` label with a 24dp caret — `DiDi y Uber · Por km ▾` on Tus apps, `Uber · CDMX · Por viaje ▾` on every Tu ciudad state, with or without a standing, so confirming a week never moves the selector; a standing sentence is plain text, never a selector). No segmented controls, no pills, no chips, no filter capsules.
- Reader state in the chrome is a **status line**: 16sp 600 words led by a mark — `Lector funcionando` by a 10dp green dot with a soft halo (the one non-verdict use of verde: "it works"), `Lector preparado` by the broken "━ ━" 24 × 2dp ink segment, `Lector apagado` / `En pausa` by the dashed one — with the time as a 400 `--ink-2` suffix ("desde las 7:50", "· última lectura ayer 20:10"). No other hue.

"Unknown" is a data state, not a fourth band. **Engine changes required before this appearance ships:** remove "missing fare → red"; remove the zero-cost fallback when marginal cost is unavailable; remove the automatic unit substitution; keep `distinctUntilChanged`.

**Placement, growth and motion.** The plate sits **just above the host's offer card**, 12dp from the right edge (top 428 on the standard 393 × 852dp host, its foot about 16dp above the card — a mock reference, not a production coordinate), so the band reads beside the offer it judges and never covers the fare, Aceptar, the countdown, the ≡ / En línea / ⌕ row or a navigation banner; a drag moves it to the left edge. The safe band is the host's own: from the bottom of its top chrome (the control row or a navigation banner) to the top of its offer card. Positions are stored **per host app and orientation** and revalidated on every layout change (tall offer C8, nav banner C9, font scale C10): the plate follows the card's top. **Suppression:** when the band between the top chrome and the card's top is shorter than the plate + 24dp, no chip is drawn (C11) rather than covering a control or shrinking; the offer is still logged in Tu día's offer list in its own row — the mark in its verdict colour, the outcome, the time, the meaning and the net rate — with the reason "sin semáforo en pantalla" on its quiet line (`[R] Se fue sin tomar / 12:10 · Abajo de tu mínimo · sin semáforo en pantalla · $2.20/km`, §5.3), so the log never implies the driver saw a chip, and Lector counts the day's suppressed offers ("Sin semáforo en pantalla hoy", §5.2). **Growth — the 1.3× font rule:** every size on the plate scales with the system font and the plate grows with it — at 1.3× it is 300 × 130 (band 73, lines 29/28, the rate 52sp, the other rate 27sp, the labels 18sp, C10) — and placement and suppression are rechecked after any growth; never truncate, ellipsize, shrink or swap a figure; money, sign and unit never separate. **Motion:** appear ≤ 150 ms after the parse — a fade with a 16dp rise over 120 ms on a decelerate curve (reduced motion: a 100 ms fade only); leave 500 ms after the same offer's card leaves, over 160 ms; a re-parse to a different state is an **atomic swap** — the band jumps to its new slot, no crossfade, no travel; a new offer or host change clears stale values immediately. No pulse, count-up, countdown, beep, haptic or celebration. The touch region is the plate minus the system's back-gesture inset, so a drag never fires Back and Back never fires a drag. **Colour-blind cue:** the band's slot and the solid cue — a deuteranope who sees verde and rojo as similar olives should still see top versus bottom and ▲ versus ▼ (R15 renders it; R14 tests it on a mount).

**Placement frames (C8–C11, the same 393 × 852dp host).** *C8 · tall offer:* the 14:32 verde offer on the host's tall card (a sheet handle, the "Viaje" capsule on the countdown ring's row, the fare with the host's own gross rate under it — `$12.33/km` = $148 ÷ 12 km, before costs — the two rows, the passenger and payment lines, Aceptar and the host's Rechazar), its top at y 313 from content, not padding; the plate rides up with the card to y 201–301, clear of the control row, the fare and both buttons; the chip never repeats the host's gross figure, and the two never share a position or a face — meant to keep Kompara's net apart from the host's gross, which R14 must confirm. *C9 · navigation banner:* at 14:50 the driver is still on the 14:32 trip when the amarillo offer arrives (`$7.16/km` neto · `$191/h`); the host's 96dp turn banner at y 84–180 ("2 min · 1.2 km") replaces the control row and the map re-frames under it; the card keeps its standard top, so the plate keeps its place above it (y 428–528), far from the banner and never on a pin; whether the ink plate groups with the host's dark banner is a mounted-test item (R14). *C10 · 1.3× font:* the 14:32 offer at system font 1.3×: the plate is **300 × 130** by the font rule; the host scales too (its card top rises) and the plate stays just above the card, y 331–461. *C11 · no safe band:* the 12:10 rojo offer ($62) arriving during navigation on the tall card — banner to 180, card from 265 — leaves 85dp where the plate needs 100 + 24: **no chip is drawn** (never over the banner, the fare or Aceptar, never shrunk to fit); the offer is still parsed and logged in Tu día as above (the 19 oct fixture has no suppressed offer, so D1 draws no such row); suppression is per layout — when the banner leaves, the band is recomputed and the chip returns above the card.

**Gestures.** **Drag only.** Drag moves the chip (snaps to the nearer edge; a drag never fires anything else). **Tap does nothing** — the parked tap-to-expand failure modes (accidental toggles while dragging, window relayout, low payoff) stand; the "why" lives in Tu día › Detalle de oferta, and the band's place against the driver's own two lines explains itself. **Long-press does nothing either.** The overlay threshold editor is **removed**: the chip exists only while an offer is on screen, so an editor over the host could only ever open during a live, ticking offer — "parked only" was impossible to honour. Thresholds are adjusted in Tu semáforo (§5.14), reached from Cuenta or from an offer's detail ("Ajustar mis límites"). This also deletes one interactive surface over someone else's app.

**Recovery.** One unreadable offer: gris, no notification. Repeated failures: the app is marked in Lector. Capture stopped while the service lives: the bounded "Lector detenido" tab (C6; 5 s, once per host foreground, non-interactive). Accessibility service dead: nothing can draw, so the watchdog notification **"Se apagó el lector en DiDi · Le falta un permiso. Revisa Kompara al estacionarte." (H6; C6's phrasing)** (one per unresolved incident, deduplicated, naming the real cause — revoked permission · stopped capture · unsupported version — each with its own recovery route; suppressed while "en pausa") + Hoy's off state (B2, an ink state); tap opens Lector, which explains the next step *before* any system prompt. **After the trip and at the end of the day.** After each trip: nothing — the ledger records silently; no toast, praise or judgment. Day summary (optional, channel "Resúmenes"): "Tu resumen de hoy está listo · 9 viajes · 8 h 10 min en turno" (H6: one duration, in D1's form; data fragments carry no closing period) — **no money in any notification** (the phone is mounted and unlocked while driving), never "turno cerrado" as a certainty, and summaries are deferred while a ride app is in the foreground **or an offer or trip is known to be active** (a navigation app can be in front during a trip). Lock-screen versions carry titles only (`setPublicVersion`).

### 9j Blind template test (Round 13–14)

**Why.** The founder's complaint — the chip "still looks like all other apps designed by Claude for that exact same concept" — had never been tested directly or blindly; Rounds 11–13 passed both reviewers on their own rubrics. Record: [`180`](ux-elevation/debate/180-blind-template-test.md), proposals `182` and `184`, propagation [`190`](ux-elevation/debate/190-blind-winners-propagation.md).

**Method.** (1) **A generic baseline:** fresh models with no project context designed the same chip and the same Hoy from one neutral brief — 12 generic chips (Opus ×4, Sonnet ×4, Haiku ×3, Codex ×1) and 4 generic Hoy screens. (2) **A neutral host:** every design rendered on one identical, unbranded host with only its own CSS; ours were injected the same way, with the Round 10 chip (the version the founder rejected) as a control. (3) **Shuffled letters,** the answer key kept outside the judges' folder. (4) **Six context-free judges** — Claude Plan agents (Opus ×2, Sonnet ×2, Haiku ×1) and Codex CLI — grouped the designs into visual families, flagged templates, and scored distinctiveness, craft, one-second glance and Uber-class rank against Uber Driver screenshots. Chip rounds 1–5, Hoy rounds 1–3. A degraded-image glance proxy (tiny, blur, greyscale, deuteranopia, glare) scored every design full marks — models read degraded images far better than peripheral human vision — so it is not used as evidence.

**Outcome.**
- **The template is real.** 11 of 12 generic chips fall into two families every judge named: the "split verdict tile" (a coloured block with a check and a verdict word, a dark panel, a red-amber-green slider with a thumb between $6 and $8) and the "financial summary card" (a status pill, a big $/km, $/h, a gauge). The Round 10 chip was called a template by 5 of 6 judges.
- **v3 was distinct, not Uber-class** (craft 5.3 against 6.2–7.5 for the generics; glance rank 10.7 of 14; Uber-class rank 10.0 of 11 in round 2): flush-edge bars and tiny grey labels read unfinished, the amarillo band was thin, and the position had to be learned, with no shape or word for a first encounter.
- **The chip winner, "la regla" (r5a, §6):** distinct 7.5 · craft 7.5 (= the best generic) · Uber-class rank **2.8 of 11, the best of all** · template votes **0/6** · point of view **5/6** · glance rank 6.2 of 11 — the first encounter still favours the template's verdict word and road-sign icons, which the mounted driver test settles (R14).
- **The Hoy winner, v5 (§5.1):** Uber-class **8.1, the best of 7** · clarity 8.3 (best generic 8.7) · distinct 7.2 · template votes **0/6** · Uber rank **1.2** (5 of 6 judges ranked it first).
- **Vocabulary, from the judges' reading:** the old pair read backwards ("tu mínimo" for the green limit), so the green limit is **"tu meta"** and the red one **"tu mínimo"** (§4).

**Propagated in this version:** la regla (§6, and every chip preview), Hoy v5 (§5.1) and its root anatomy on every period hero (§4), the white day surface and the system layer (§4), the new pair everywhere. §9g–§9i keep the raya-con-carril chip and the old pair as they were recorded.

The same value goes into `design-system/tokens` before the chip is built. **v3:** the product palette is the paper/ink set of §4 (`--paper` — white `#FFFFFF` by day since Round 14 — `--paper-2`, `--ink`/`-2`/`-3`, `--rule`, the verdict triple, `--chip-ink`, `--k`), implemented in `base-v3.css` and to be mirrored in `design-system/tokens` and `theme/Color.kt`; the slate and emerald scales, `chipNeutral`, info-blue, amber and the second red are deleted, not deprecated.
- **Chip geometry (la regla):** one `Regla` component (`.regla`; exact markup per state in `_regla-markup.html`): a 240 × 100 ink plate, radius 14; two **categorical** labelled lines — tu meta upper, tu mínimo lower, never proportional to the money; the trip as a full-width band whose **slot** is the verdict (above both lines · between · below both; none on gris) with the solid ▲ ▬ ▼ cue and the rate always at the band's left. The position comes from a **per-host validated safe band** (the bottom of the host's top chrome to the top of its offer card): the plate sits just above the card, 12dp from the right edge, stored per host app and orientation and revalidated on every layout change; **suppression** when that band is shorter than the plate + 24dp (the offer is still logged in Tu día with the reason "sin semáforo en pantalla" and counted in Lector's "Sin semáforo en pantalla hoy"); one geometry for every chip (the overlay, the A1/B3/G2 previews and the night host; frames add only a position); **every size scales with the system font** and the plate grows with it (1.3× → 300 × 130, C10) — `VerdictChipUi`'s 232dp step and the K are retired; state changes are atomic swaps; C6 is a separate 240 × 56dp non-interactive ink tab with no lines and no band. - **Fonts:** bundle Barlow 400/600 and Barlow Semi Condensed 500/600/700 as app assets; a build-time check that the shipped files carry `tnum` and `lnum` and a device check of 13sp Barlow at 1×; fallback Roboto (UI) and Archivo SemiCondensed 600 (money); never horizontal scaling; no weight 800/900; Inter removed.
- **Theme:** follow the system (`isSystemInDarkTheme()`) with a persisted Cuenta override "Tema · Automático / Claro / Oscuro"; no Material You dynamic colour; the overlay ignores the theme — chip ink and the verdict triple are fixed in both modes.
- **The mark:** one `Mark` composable (16 × 24dp, 18 × 28 in `OfferRow`; states v/a/r/g) is the only leading glyph in offer rows (Hoy, Tu día) and is drawn in ink in Comparar; no icon tiles, circled glyphs or coloured dots anywhere in rows; `VerdictBadge` is removed.

- **Golden fixture + CI:** one fixture (costs, floors, a week of offers/shifts, lo del coche, a cohort with its basis) drives every mock, preview, demo and screenshot; a check fails when a displayed number differs from engine output or when two screens show the same period in different scopes without a label.

- **Capture lanes:** one disclosure covering both; consent prompts only where the device actually needs them (API < 30 or a disabled silent lane) — recovery follows capability, not API level alone.
- **Costs:** typical defaults labelled and surfaced in the hero's quiet line; effective-dated cost changes; a weekly fixed-cost deduction that is actually applied to week/month (replace the unread `dailyFixedCosts`).
- **Trial:** a server-granted promotional entitlement separate from Play subscriptions; explicit start; no automatic conversion. Validate against Play's payments policy.
- **Verification:** eligibility checked before the sales action; cached positive entitlement survives fetch failures; "Importa una semana para desbloquear" removed as a post-purchase state.
- **Import:** consent step on every route including `ShareImportActivity`; one parse; reconciliation by app/period/field instead of replacement; retention wording tied to the backend's actual behaviour.

- **Notifications:** channels "Estado del lector" and "Resúmenes"; one deduplicated incident notification naming the cause, suppressed while paused; no amounts in any notification; summaries deferred while a ride app is foreground; `setPublicVersion` with titles only.
- **Overlay:** drag only (inert tap and long-press); per-host persisted position with revalidation; suppression when no safe position fits; the "Lector detenido" tab (C6) is non-interactive and time-bounded; no interactive surface over the host without an offer.
- **Data flows and consent:** the contribution opt-in (default off) gates any upload of weekly aggregates to the benchmark population; the anonymous parse-success telemetry is either disclosed (as now) or removed; account deletion cancels pending uploads, revokes the opt-in, and has an external request route (kompara.mx/borrar); the Data-safety form lists derived totals as transmitted when an account exists.
