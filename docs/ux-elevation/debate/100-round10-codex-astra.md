## 6. What only evidence can settle

- **One-second comprehension:** timed mounted-phone exposures at 60cm, day/night and with colour-vision differences; include negative values, neutral states and enlarged fonts.
- **Second-rate usefulness:** compare one-rate and two-rate chips on decision accuracy and response time.
- **Capture reliability and placement:** replay supported host layouts and full shifts, measuring latency, stale values, covered controls, permission loss and pause/resume.
- **Install-to-value:** time fresh stock/OEM installs through “preparado”, including settings return and skipped signup.
- **Money comprehension:** ask drivers to explain B1/E1/E3/F2, distinguishing offer estimates, statement payments, deductions and partial-month coverage.
- **Typical-cost accuracy:** measure verdict changes when drivers replace defaults with their actual costs.
- **Accounting and entitlement continuity:** replay cancellations, duplicate imports, corrections, month boundaries, pending payments and failed renewals with banked promotional days.
- **Native usability:** verify Compose target bounds, font scaling, focus order and tab findability on representative phones.
- **Legal/disclosure validity:** validate B-038’s sources, income basis and actual data flows before enabling dependent claims.
- **Premium demand:** observe repeated weekly imports and willingness to pay for the comparison actually available.
