## 5. The one change that would raise the score the most

**Make evidence eligibility part of every calculation, before formatting or selling the result.** One set of dated records must carry outcome, source, cost version, verification and coverage, then generate the shift, week, month, comparison, import preview and share card. Missing expenses must withhold the full net; unverified payments must withhold a verification-dependent standing; undated income must remain unallocated. Generate the specification examples from those same records. This would repair the largest remaining trust failures simultaneously; another layer of “aprox.” copy will not.

## 6. Positions you still hold against the synthesis

- **One deciding rate remains the better live-chip default.** C1–C3 retain the second rate without evidence that it improves one-second decisions; C5 now sacrifices its net qualifier to keep that information.
- **Hoy should be smaller.** B1’s observation and three offer rows still contradict the explicit attention budget. Status, the qualified daily result and access to Tu día are sufficient.
- **A disclaimer cannot repair incompatible financial evidence.** H5b’s missing-cost net and E5’s unchanged payment-based bars remain wrong design choices even when labelled approximate or placed behind a future gate.
