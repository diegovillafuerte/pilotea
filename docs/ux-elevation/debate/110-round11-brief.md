# Round 11 brief — "not a template": signature, craft, radical simplicity

**Date:** 2026-10-26 · **Mode:** design only, nothing is coded · **Reviewers:** Codex CLI (GPT-6 Astra, ultra) and Claude Opus 5.5, independently, same brief → cross-critique → synthesis (Claude Fable 5.1) → rebuild → re-score, repeated until both PASS on both rubrics.

## Why this round exists

Rounds 1–10 (`docs/ux-elevation/debate/`) took Kompara v2 from 51/80 to Opus 69 / Codex 68 on the structural rubric (`02-rubric-uber-bar.md`): one job per screen, one primary, numbers that reconcile, every state designed. The founder looked at the result on 2026-10-26 and said:

> "Look at the entire app and product and elevate the entire concept of the app, make sure the UX is much easier to use and that each screen is very simple and easy to understand. Do not stop until you reach a version that is comparable in use and feel to the benchmarks of the market or even an app like Uber and the other competitors we have benchmarked. **For example, the design of the chip still looks like all other apps designed by Claude for that exact same concept.**"

He is right. Put the rendered frames next to the Uber Driver, DiDi Conductor and GigU store screenshots and the verdict is immediate: Kompara v2 is a rigorous *wireframe* in the default "AI dark dashboard" dialect, and its chip is the category's default coloured badge (GigU's overlay is the same object with a green border). Rubric `03-rubric-r11-craft.md` adds the missing criteria 9–12 (signature idea · glance physics · craft · radical simplicity). From now on a version passes only on BOTH rubrics.

## What the synthesis lead (Fable) already sees — challenge it, don't rubber-stamp it

Visual system: Tailwind slate-900/800 surfaces + emerald-600 + Inter 900 heroes **inside cards** + circled-glyph rows + pills for every selector + segmented controls + bottom nav = the template. Uber puts the number on the surface, not in a card; Uber is white with black type and one accent; DiDi is orange with giant numbers; both have almost no explanatory copy.

Copy density: ten rounds of reviewer-proofing wrote every caveat on screen. B1 says the same qualifier three times ("Te quedan hoy · aprox." / "6 viajes tomados" / "Viajes tomados según la oferta, después de gasolina y desgaste · aprox." / "Cómo se calculó"). E1 stacks nine elements (title, segmented, stepper, hero, 3-line scope, car-cost row, bars, compare card, Ver-cada-día row, share button) where Uber Earnings has one number, one chart, one list. E4 is two paragraphs. F1–F5 have segmented + stepper + three pills + sentence + rows + caveat + rows + button.

Chip: coloured rounded rectangle, white check in a translucent circle, 30sp number, 15sp second line, 16dp "K" square. It has to be *read*; the colour is a fill the eye parses, not a signal the periphery catches. The host mock (white bars on a grid) looks fake and makes the chip look like a toast over a wireframe.

## What you must deliver (write it as a markdown report; numbered sections exactly as below)

1. **Diagnosis — why it reads as a template.** Concrete, visual-system element by element and then the ten worst screens, each with the one thing that gives it away. Say which of Fable's observations above are wrong or incomplete.
2. **The signature idea.** Propose **three** candidate visual concepts for Kompara, each derived from what the product uniquely does (net after *your* costs vs *your* floor; it only informs). For each: one-sentence pitch a design director would use; an ASCII sketch of the chip in verde / amarillo / rojo / gris at the real footprint (≤ 200×88dp at 1×; text ≥ 13sp; no verdict word; glyph language allowed but not required; no brand strip; drag only); how the same idea carries into Hoy, Tu día, Ganancias and Comparar (one line each); risks against the 1-second mounted glance, occlusion of the host's fare/controls, colour-vision deficiency, Play's accessibility-tool heuristics, and the "looks like a control" trap. Then **pick one** and argue it over the other two. Avoid: a literal traffic light, a speedometer gauge, a circular progress ring, a coloured rounded card — all four are the template too.
3. **Visual system v3.** Palette (the verdict triple keeps its semantics and its contrast ratios; everything else is open, including abandoning slate/emerald and dark-first), type (which face for money, which for UI, scale with at most five steps), surface model (what replaces cards-in-cards), iconography, motion rules, and a host mock that looks like a real ride app without any platform mark. Give concrete token values and say what they replace in `design-system/ui_kits/android-app-v2/base.css`.
4. **Radical simplification, screen by screen.** For A1, A2, A5, B1, B2, B3, C1–C6, D1, D2, E1, E3, E4, F1, F2, H2b: the one sentence a driver would use to explain the screen; the elements to delete; the elements that stay; an ASCII wireframe (≤ 14 lines) with the real fixture strings (the checker asserts them, keep every number exactly; copy may move, be cut or be merged but must not change a figure); the copy, reduced, in es-MX with `tú`. Rule of thumb: a qualifier is said once per screen, ideally by position or colour, never by a sentence.
5. **Score the current version on criteria 9–12** (`03-rubric-r11-craft.md`) with the hard-fail check, and confirm in one line each whether criteria 1–8 still hold at ≥ 8 (they did in Round 10). Then say what score your own proposal would reach and what evidence would be needed to believe it.
6. **What must not change.** The locked decisions (3 tabs, reader = status, drag-only chip, gris never red, no auto-action, no platform logos, "Te quedan" + one qualifier, optional signup, free IMSS review, fixture arithmetic, legal gate B-038) stay unless you argue, explicitly as a fork, that one of them is wrong under the new bar. The "K" square is NOT sacred: the brand mark may be reinterpreted (the brand is the letter K and the green; the current square is a placeholder).

Write for a principal designer who will rebuild the mockups from your report. Be specific enough that two people would draw the same thing. Do not pad; do not restate the rubric.
