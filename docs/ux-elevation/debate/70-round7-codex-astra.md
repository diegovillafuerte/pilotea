## 6. What only evidence can settle

- **One-second chip comprehension:** test C1–C5 at 60 cm on a mount, day/night and with colour-vision differences; measure correct floor decisions and false rejection of gris.
- **Whether the second rate helps:** compare one-rate and two-rate chips under the same timed exposures; measure accuracy and decision time.
- **“Te quedan” and source comprehension:** ask ten drivers to explain B1, E1, E8 and F2, including estimates, expenses, statement payment and waiting time.
- **Install→preparado time:** measure fresh installs on supported stock/OEM phones, including settings return, conditional battery recovery and skipped account.
- **Trustworthy reader status:** replay revoked permissions, process death, repeated parse failures, partial support and pause/resume; verify B2/B5/B6/G4 against actual service state.
- **Safe overlay placement and legibility:** test real supported host layouts at enlarged text sizes; verify no covered controls, stale values or clipped units.
- **Financial reconciliation:** replay cancellation/undo, duplicate imports, equal-count but different-trip coverage, missing kilometres and month-boundary imports; verify every resulting period total and source.
- **Benchmark validity:** provide the cohort distribution and eligibility rules, then independently reproduce the 78th percentile and each displayed sample size.
- **Typical-cost suitability:** compare initial-cost verdicts with drivers’ confirmed costs and measure verdict flips.
- **Billing promises:** test grace, hold, pending, cancellation and offline entitlement in Play; verify H2b’s native purchase sheet and first charge honour remaining trial/referral access.
- **IMSS accuracy:** validate the applicable components, factor and dated reference against official sources and real cases; retain E4 until that gate passes.
- **Rendered accessibility:** inspect 48dp hit areas, focus order, scrolling and enlarged-text sheets in Compose; corrected CSS alone cannot establish native behaviour.
