## 5. The one change that would raise the score the most

Make every earnings hero derive from **dated records with an explicit payment outcome, expense version and source coverage**. The golden fixture must include those records—not just matching headline examples—and produce the offer, day, week, month, import result and exported card. When completion, distance or calendar allocation is unsupported, render that missing-evidence state instead of a net result. This directly fixes the impossible month, estimated offers counted as paid, capture-gap contradictions and imported totals with unknown expenses: the remaining defects most likely to destroy a driver’s trust.

## 6. Positions you still hold against the synthesis

- **One deciding rate on the live chip until testing justifies two.** C1–C3 no longer calculate incorrectly, but the second rate still consumes attention without supplied evidence that it improves one-second decisions.
- **A smaller first viewport on Hoy and one expression of a standing.** B1’s numerical observation and offer rates, and F2’s repeated percentile, still contradict the explicit attention budget.
- **Allocate fixed expenses, not undated income.** §5.7’s proportional weekly-income rule is not evidence of what a driver earned during a calendar month.
