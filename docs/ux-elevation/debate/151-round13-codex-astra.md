## 6. What only evidence can settle

- **Mounted recognition:** test day/night state identification, particularly amarillo, gris and C6, at the specified distance and eccentricity. Retain the ≥95% identification and zero gris-as-rojo criteria.
- **Teaching and money comprehension:** test A1 without coaching; G2’s selectable boundaries; E1’s retained-money chart; C8’s gross/net distinction; and H5b’s week/month consequence.
- **Real-host coexistence:** replay actual Uber/DiDi layouts, banners and font scales. Measure suppression frequency as well as overlap, and confirm suppressed offers still enter history.
- **Native behaviour and rendering:** verify hit regions, font files, small-text legibility, drag versus system Back, inert taps, stopped-tab touch-through, stale-value clearing and atomic state changes.
- **Setup and deployment gates:** measure install-to-first-value time and night glare; complete the already specified review of disclosure and billing copy. The renders establish neither duration nor approval.
