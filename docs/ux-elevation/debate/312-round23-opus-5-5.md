## 1. The yellow state: adopt **"Supera tu mínimo"**

- **Where it changes:**
  - the chip phrase (C2, C3 and the night twins)
  - A1's legend row
  - Tu día rows: «14:50 · Supera tu mínimo»
  - the spoken label: «Supera tu mínimo: 7.16 pesos netos por km»
  - D2c's line becomes **«$7.16/km · supera tu mínimo de $6.00/km»**, matching D2's «arriba de tu meta de $8.00/km».
- **Why:**
  - It states the one comparison that settles the decision: the floor is met. All judges worked that out on their own in runs 5–7.
  - It drops the second comparison: j2 called the phrase "a mouthful" and j1 "slow for a slow reader".
  - It ends j4's "meta before mínimo reads backwards".
  - The first words stay distinct (Arriba / Supera / Abajo), and it is 16 characters instead of 25.
- **Alternatives rejected:**
  - «Pasa…» was already rejected in Round 21 because it reads as *pásala*.
  - «Arriba de tu mínimo» starts with the same word as the green state.
  - «Sobre tu mínimo» reads as "about your minimum" on a label.
- **Risks, both handed to R14:**
  - It is a verb where the other two phrases are prepositions, so it could read as a command («¡Supera tu mínimo!»).
  - At exactly $6.00 it is as loose as «Arriba de tu meta» already is at $8.00.
  - R14 keeps the after-A1 probe «¿Qué quiere decir el amarillo?» and scores the state, accepting «entre» or «supera».
- **Still not a verdict word.** That choice stays the founder's (R14).
- **Expected effect:** task 2 from 5.67 to about 5.9. j2, j4 and j5 may still ask for a word, and only R14 can answer that.

## 2. Trip versus day: neither (b) nor (c)

**(b) "Repartido (6 viajes) ~$78" is rejected.** Splitting a fixed cost per trip does three bad things:
- A past trip's figure changes during the day (about $78 at 14:40, about $90 at 21:10).
- A 4 km green trip is charged the same $36 as a 12 km one. A short «Arriba de tu meta» trip would then show about $2, a chip-versus-sheet contradiction that rule 5 would catch.
- It brings back the per-trip car charge that Round 17 ruled out.

**(c) Tagging every figure «con coche» / «sin coche» is rejected.** It puts a qualifier on every line, which rubric 12 names as a template smell. The labels already name the basis. In run 7, j2–j5 checked both figures and found them correctly labelled; what they want is the renter's number next to the trip.

**Decision for D2, D2b, D2c and D2d.** The 13sp note «Lo del coche ($214 al día) se resta de tu día, no de cada viaje.» becomes a body-size row. Its chevron opens that day's breakdown (D3a at 14:40, D3 at 21:10):
- **«Llevas hoy, ya con lo del coche: $548»** ›, or **$1,026** on the 21:10 frames
- sub-line, 13sp ink-2: **«Se resta por día ($214), no por viaje»**
- for a trip on a closed day: **«Ese día te quedaron, ya con lo del coche: $X»**

**Why:**
- The renter's real question is about la cuenta (j1: «para tu cuenta de hoy…»), and that is a fact about the day.
- The two verbs now sit side by side, each with its basis: «Te dejó antes de lo del coche $114» and «Llevas hoy, ya con lo del coche $548».
- It is the same figure as on Hoy, so there is no new number and no new basis.
- It answers "explained only in small text" (j2, j3, j4).

**Add one probe to R17.** Show D2 and ask «¿Cuánto te dejó este viaje? ¿Y cuánto llevas hoy ya con lo del coche?». It passes if at least 8 of 10 name $114 and $548. If 3 or more of the 5 renters still ask for the trip's figure after the car, the per-trip question goes to the founder with real evidence.

**Expected effect:** named by 4/6 now, by 2/6 or fewer after; task 9 from 6.00 to about 6.2.

## 5. The test itself

- **Match the personas to the fixture, keep their traits.**
  - All six drive Uber and DiDi, mostly Uber (the fixture has 34 Uber trips and 28 DiDi).
  - All six already entered Lo del coche at $1,500 a week (la cuenta for renters, crédito y seguro for owners).
  - Rosa and Ana lose "Uber only". Jorge loses "DiDi mostly", which also clashes with «Abrir Uber para probar».
  - Age, comfort with apps, colour blindness, large text and impatience stay as they are.
- **Same scenario in the real-driver plan**, because that cohort includes Uber-only drivers. The line read after task 8 becomes: **«Ahora imagina que ya llevas semanas usando Kompara. En esta prueba manejas Uber y DiDi, más Uber, y ya le pusiste lo que pagas del coche: $1,500 a la semana.»**
- **Task 7's wording contradicts its world.** The screen says 8:01 and «desde ayer 20:31»; the task says "durante tu turno" (j2 noticed). New wording, in TASKS.md and the plan: **«Empiezas tu turno temprano y el semáforo no sale en tus ofertas. Averigua por qué y qué hacer.»**
- **For a model, task 2 is a reading task, not a glance**, because the harness prints the chip's text. In task 2, print no screen text, only the screenshot. Its score still never picks the phrase; R14 does.
- **Model scores are compressed.**
  - In run 7 only 6 of 54 ratings were 7, five of them on task 1, which needs no taps.
  - Any task with a tap scores 5–6, because the brief asks for hesitations on every task.
  - So a mean of 6.0 is a ceiling set by how the judges rate, not a design threshold.
  - The judges' Uber scores are estimates from two screenshots, not a baseline.
- **Run 8 will not be strictly comparable to runs 5–7.** The personas, task 7's wording and task 2's harness all change. That is acceptable for a readiness check.

## 6. Run 8, and what counts as "comparable"

- **Do one run 8, then stop model walkthroughs.**
  - Its job is to confirm on the corrected test that Round 23 clears the repeated trip-versus-day defect and the new «Por app» jump.
  - Don't chase 6.0, and don't do a run 9 whatever the mean.
  - The model walkthrough has shown what it can: every answer screen reached in three straight runs, almost no misses, no task under 5.5, and money problems down to one undated line.
  - What remains — a verdict word, the per-trip car cost, the Android settings step, fear of a ban — only R14, R17 and the real phone can answer.
- **I retire the gate I set in `252` (mean ≥ 6.0, vs Uber ≥ 7.5) for model runs.** The design is ready for real drivers when run 8 shows:
  1. 54/54 answer screens reached, with misses ≤ 0.2 per task;
  2. no task under 5.5;
  3. no defect in the top three of 4 or more judges in both run 7 and run 8;
  4. no money contradiction that is actually true of the fixture;
  5. Kompara at or above the judges' own Uber estimate on tasks 1, 4 and 9;
  6. B1's $548 read as after the car by 6/6.

  The mean (I expect about 6.0 ± 0.2) and the vs-Uber score are reported, not gated.

  - Kompara within 0.5 of the same drivers' own Uber ease scores on tasks 1, 4 and 9;
  - a mean of at least 6.0 over the nine tasks.
