## 2. Hard fails

- **Auto-action proposed: none.** A3 explicitly says “nunca acepta ni rechaza por ti”; “Aceptar” in C1–C5 belongs to the generic host app.
- **Platform logos: none.** Platform names are plain text; the overlay uses Kompara’s K.
- **Driving-time glance: FAIL — C2.** The displayed fare, distance and configured costs produce approximately **$4.43/km**, below C5’s **$6.00/km** red floor, yet the chip shows **yellow, $7.10/km**. The immediate answer is wrong. This is demonstrable from the fixture, without claiming a device-test result.
- **Paywall blanks the free product: none.** F3 retains the driver’s value; E2 locks an older period while recent history remains free.
- **Onboarding screen with more than one ask: none.** A2 asks one cost question; A3 one consent; A4 one permission handoff. Alternative actions and skip links are not additional asks.

## 5. The one change that would raise the score the most

Make **one trip and one week reconcile everywhere**. The offer, chip, saved detail, daily total, weekly total, comparison and shared card must use the same supported inputs, expense scope, dates and outcome states. Where evidence is insufficient, show an estimate or unavailable result instead of a definitive conclusion. That single change removes the wrong live verdict, contradictory earnings, invalid ranking and misleading exports—the defects that most directly destroy trust in this product.

## 6. Positions you still hold against the synthesis

- **One deciding rate on the live chip.** C2/C5 add a second rate and an unexplained ▼. Keep the selected rate and “neto aprox.”; put the other rate in detail unless testing establishes a benefit.
- **A smaller Hoy.** B1’s observation and three offers break the numerical discipline that the daily hero needs. Keep status, qualified daily result and a route to Tu día.
- **Assumed costs must remain visibly assumed.** A2’s defaults do not justify A1’s “de verdad” or unqualified personal net figures throughout the first shift.
- **Matching comparison bases is mandatory.** §5.9 deliberately permits the mismatch visible in F2. A footnote cannot make net and before-expenses earnings comparable.
