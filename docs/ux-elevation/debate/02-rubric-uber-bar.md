# The "Uber-class" bar — scoring rubric for Kompara's redesign

Benchmark = the Uber Driver app (and the discipline of DiDi Driver / GigU's rebuilt 2024 app). The bar is
structural, not brand: we steal the *discipline*, not the look.

Score each criterion 1–10. A proposal "passes" when every criterion is ≥ 8 and the total ≥ 68/80.

| # | Criterion | What a 10 looks like (the Uber reference) | What a 5 looks like |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **One job per screen** | Every screen answers exactly one driver question; the hero (one number or one status) is unmistakable at 60 cm; everything else is support. Uber Home = map + GO + today's earnings pill. | Two or three competing heroes, cards of equal weight, a scroll of sections. |
| 2 | **One primary action** | A single filled button per screen, placed where the thumb is (bottom), labeled with a verb. Uber: GO / Accept / Cash out. | Several filled buttons, or the main action is a text link, or hidden in a card. |
| 3 | **Navigation that disappears** | ≤ 4 tabs (Uber: Home · Earnings · Inbox · Account) or 3; the driver never wonders "where is X"; settings and rarely-used tools are tucked away behind Account. | 5 tabs with overlapping meanings; a "feature" (the reader) as a tab; drill-ins three levels deep. |
| 4 | **Numbers discipline** | Pesos formatted es-MX, tabular, one unit per number, ≤ 3 numbers in the first viewport; percentiles/standings as one phrase. Uber Earnings: one big number, one chart, breakdown behind a tap. | Grids of 5–6 metric tiles, mixed units, labels that clip, the same metric on three screens. |
| 5 | **Copy** | Short verb phrases, `tú`, zero internal vocabulary, every string earns its place; the colour or the number says it and the word is cut. | Explanatory paragraphs, "lector de pantalla", redundant sublabels, English-isms. |
| 6 | **States are designed** | Empty, loading, degraded (reader off / permission revoked), locked (paywall), error — each is on-brand, says what to do next in one line, and never blanks the screen. | Happy path only; spinners with text; a blank card when there is no data. |
| 7 | **Time-to-value** | First value < 60 s of install: a live or simulated verdict before any account or permission ask that isn't strictly required; permission asks framed in context, one at a time. | 7+ onboarding steps, signup before any value, permission walls explained with paragraphs. |
| 8 | **Consistency & feel** | One component vocabulary (rows, sheets, pills), one spacing rhythm, restrained motion, dark-first, tap targets ≥ 48dp; the app feels like one hand made it. | Mixed card weights, cards-in-cards, different padding per screen, decorative colour. |

Hard fails (any one = not comparable, regardless of score): auto-action proposed · platform logos ·
a screen that fails the 1-second glance test for a driving user · a paywall that blanks the free
experience · an onboarding step with more than one ask.
