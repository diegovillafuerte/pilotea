1. **High (P1) — Split-screen DiDi content creates a paid phantom Uber offer.** [OfferCardSelector.kt:38](/Users/panama/pilotea-v3/android/ocr/src/main/java/mx/kompara/ocr/OfferCardSelector.kt:38)  
   The OS package selects the parser, but OCR still covers the entire display.

   **Reproduction:** place the existing DiDi **$132.59** fixture in the upper split-screen pane, omit its status-clock OCR block, and show Uber’s idle `Buscando viajes` below with Uber foreground. No synthetic accept button is needed. The compiled pipeline emits a confident **UBER 132.59** offer with DiDi’s **1.2/17.3 km** legs and persists a PENDING ledger row. No Uber offer exists.

   **Fix:** associate OCR with the selected OS window’s bounds and exclude other windows/overlays. Withhold attribution when pixel ownership is ambiguous.

2. **High (P1) — Back-to-back offers inherit old money or overwrite the previous ledger offer.** [OcrOfferIdentity.kt:83](/Users/panama/pilotea-v3/android/ocr/src/main/java/mx/kompara/ocr/OcrOfferIdentity.kt:83), [line 115](/Users/panama/pilotea-v3/android/ocr/src/main/java/mx/kompara/ocr/OcrOfferIdentity.kt:115)  
   Reappearance clears the absence streak. An ordinary replacement retaining the same product, action and presentation supplies only one replacement signal: route.

   **Reproduction:** **79.45 → clear idle frame → 147.46**, at 900 ms intervals, without accessibility card semantics. With the trip changed from **2.4 to 6.3 km**, five subsequent frames retain the original UUID, **79.45**, and **2.4 km**. With identical legs, the first new frame shows **79.45**; the second confirms **147.46** and overwrites the original DAO row, retaining its original start timestamp.

   **Fix:** preserve a clear disappearance/lifecycle boundary separately from durable ledger retention. Reappearance after that boundary needs fresh identity/payment. Distinguish clear absence from OCR dropout, and suppress inherited money while replacement identity remains unresolved.

3. **Medium (P2) — A first-frame heading dropout leaves the chip overlapping the close control indefinitely.** [HostGeometryDeriver.kt:89](/Users/panama/pilotea-v3/android/parsers/src/main/java/mx/kompara/parsers/model/HostGeometryDeriver.kt:89), [OverlayPosition.kt:82](/Users/panama/pilotea-v3/android/overlay/src/main/java/mx/kompara/overlay/OverlayPosition.kt:82)  
   **Reproduction:** start the $79.45 S25 fixture without `Uber Priority`, `Exclusivo`, and `X`, with no accessibility geometry. Restore all original blocks on four subsequent captures.

   All five compiled frames place the chip at **(324,338)–(1044,674)**, overlapping close **(837,650)–(983,793)** by **24 px** vertically. Recovery remains false: the chip masks the restored heading/close blocks, while the unchanged fare incorrectly validates the upper boundary.

   **Fix:** require validated upper-card/control evidence before initial placement. A stable fare cannot establish that the region above it is safe; missing initial heading/control evidence must trigger unobscured recovery.

4. **Medium (P2) — Briefly leaving and returning duplicates an unchanged offer.** [OfferFramePipeline.kt:246](/Users/panama/pilotea-v3/android/ocr/src/main/java/mx/kompara/ocr/OfferFramePipeline.kt:246)  
   Known app departure invokes `onCaptureEnd`, immediately resetting durable identity without corroborating card disappearance.

   **Reproduction:** observe a **147.46** card, execute the app-departure path, then return to the unchanged card **800 ms later**. The compiled pipeline/recorder/lifecycle replay creates UUID B and a second row: **A 147.46 UNKNOWN → B 147.46 PENDING**.

   **Fix:** hide immediately on foreground departure while retaining suspended per-host offer identity. Resume or retire it using observed card/lifecycle evidence rather than treating lost visibility as confirmed disappearance.

The first clear absence emitted immediate `LayoutUncertain`, which detaches the chip; the proposed 1.8–2.5-second display lag was not reproduced. No separate timestamp, post-disappearance fare-write, or privacy/read-only regression was established.
