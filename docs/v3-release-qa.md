## 0.2.9 / 11 — OS attribution and corroborated offer sessions (2026-10-29)

Base **`327ac02` (0.2.8)**, branch `feat/v3-redesign`. All changes remain in the working tree; no staging or commit. This model supersedes 0.2.8's immediate structural replacement and signature-gap resets.

**Model:**

- **OS attribution:** only the accessibility service's active-window package selects Uber or DiDi. Background target events cannot override the active root. Both capture lanes check the live host before capture and after recognition; projection uses an owner-scoped accessibility probe, never cached/OCR host inference. Unknown root visibility skips capture, hides the chip and breaks candidate agreement while retaining the session; known nonhost, screen-off or capture shutdown closes observation. DiDi excludes inline and split `/km` rates from both headline and action-button payment extraction.
- **Durable presence:** one UUID and epoch start time per observed offer. Disappearance requires **at least 3 signature-free frames spanning at least 1,800 ms**, or a current explicit idle accessibility layout. The silent lane waits **700 ms after processing**; at 900 ms/frame the absence observations occur at 900/1,800/2,700 ms. Elapsed time or a countdown alone cannot prove disappearance.
- **Replacement:** at least two independent positive changes must each agree across two consecutive observations. Signals include action, product, incompatible explicit presentation labels and a complete changed route. Stacked selection requires both an independent action-control count decrease and correspondence with a previously observed secondary card band. Geometry movement, missing labels, one changed route, partial/malformed routes, ambiguous stacked selection, masks and OCR/OS dropouts are **UNCERTAIN**, retain the UUID and break candidate agreement. Additive labels are retained independently of variant priority.
- **Payment:** the first confident amount is immediate; a revision needs two consecutive agreeing confident readings within **1,500 ms**. Uncertainty retains confirmed payment and route semantics, with current-frame placement evidence. It cannot mint a UUID or ledger offer, reset first-payment allowance or supply a revision vote. Both the overlay and lifecycle use the same session ID and confirmed amount; records keep epoch timestamps. Only corroborated replacement or ended observation clears payment.
- **Placement:** current accessibility card/control geometry always constrains placement. OCR evidence under the chip is unknown. Unobscured anchors and controls survive partial frames; uncertain correspondence or a disappearing close control detaches the chip for an unobscured capture when no safe placement is proved. `LayoutUncertain` exposes content without evaluating/writing another offer or resetting identity; foreign recovery events cannot cancel the departed host's hide. After recovery, a still-safe placement stays fixed.

**Coherent model:**

- **Instance identity:** one observed card lifetime owns one UUID, assigned before payment confirmation. Parsers provide recognized product/action tokens, selected heading/action bounds and visible card-band count. A changed known host, variant, product or action, or independently anchored geometry beyond **max(24px, 2.5% viewport width)** starts a new instance immediately. Anchor movement is compared to the instance's original anchors, preventing gradual drift through the tolerance. Active stacked-card switches are detected by selected-card evidence; losing only an inactive card clears payment confidence without duplicating the active record. Matching numeric legs never establish continuity. Unknown fields keep the durable ID; two consecutive agreeing changed complete routes within 1500ms corroborate replacement when structural tokens remain unchanged. A signature-free gap uses conservative **N=1**; capture end and an explicit same-frame idle host-layout observation also end continuity. Missing accessibility node bounds alone remain unknown, because supported Uber layouts often expose no card nodes.
- **Confirmed payment versus candidates:** the parser remains fail-safe, so the **first confident payment of a new instance displays immediately** to preserve first-reveal latency. After that, revision requires **two consecutive readings with the same amount and payment basis within 1500ms**. Unreadable, invalid, rejected or unparsed intervening frames break agreement; expiry never promotes a lone candidate. Alternating 79.45/99.45 cannot change the chip or emit corrections. Only confirmed payment survives unreadable fares. Missing/changed route confidence clears payment before outlier rejection but does not create a UUID or restore the first-reveal allowance; reacquisition requires agreement.
- **Ledger separation:** both overlay and lifecycle paths receive the same instance UUID and confirmed payment. `OfferRecorder` retains its existing deduplication, pending-only/newer-observation guards, complete-route revision checks and frozen cost/threshold evaluation. The DAO stores the last confirmed history when current confidence is lost; `OverlayController.evaluate` does not rehydrate that historical fare into a live frame that withheld payment. Confirmation windows use monotonic time; session/event/ledger timestamps use capture epoch time. No retained fare is published on absent frames.
- **Placement:** derived geometry now carries bounds-only mask/upper-anchor/route provenance. When coherent route displacement exceeds **8dp** and upper anchors disappear under the chip mask, the controller hides the old footprint and uses regular placement, ignoring both transient and saved positions. Inferred whitespace cannot prove the old footprint safe. Authoritative host-layout or independently measured card bounds remain trusted. `Coincidir` participates in card anchoring and protected controls. The five-frame legacy `Viaje:` S25 replay starts at **(324,221)–(1044,557)**, grows upward 250px with close at **(837,400)–(983,543)**, and relocates clear of both close and Aceptar on every frame.
