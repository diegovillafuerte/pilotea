Thirty-eight screens

The whole app for launch: one chip, three tabs, and what sits behind the gear. It takes the place of the 108 screens in the v3 kit.

  • The chip is a colour and a numberKompara's K leads it, as a signature. A small ▲ ◆ ▼ repeats the colour for drivers who cannot tell red from green, and the unit says neto or bruto. Nothing on it repeats the verdict or the driver's own settings.
  • Three tabs, one per jobGanancias, Comparar, IMSS. The semáforo's health is one line at the top of Ganancias.
  • One meaning for every pesoWhat the trips paid, minus gasoline and wear. No figure includes the car, so none has to say whether it does.
  • The car is a weekly billOne bar on the week. First you cover it, then the rest is yours.
  • Estimates are explained onceIn Cómo se calculó, one tap from the number, and not on every label.
  • New since the reviewGanancias has two periods, Hoy and Semana. The month lives in the IMSS tab, the one place a driver needs it.

The semáforo, over the ride app

One pill, 56 dp tall, 12 dp above the offer and flush with its left edge. Kompara's green K rides in an ink head, as on the app icon. It is the same on every offer and never sits on the verdict colour itself. Then colour, then the rate, with the word neto or bruto under the unit, whichever the driver chose. On a name-your-price offer the chip grows a tray with the counter-offers that would reach the driver's two limits. The ride apps are redrawn here without their brands; the second one stands for an app with a dark card and its own decline control.

IMSS

The third tab answers one question: will I make it this month. The rule, as the founder states it: take what an app paid in the month (trips, incentives and referral incentives, without tips), take off the cost deduction (48 % for a vehicle today) and then IVA; if what is left reaches CDMX's monthly minimum wage, the driver qualifies for full coverage. For 2026 that means $21,938 paid by one app. The driver sees that one amount and a bar toward it, never the percentages. The three numbers behind it come from remote config, because they change.

What waits on you

The set is built: version 0.3.0, on branch feat/v4-simple-launch, not yet on a phone. Thirty-seven of the thirty-eight frames are in the app. What is still open:

  • Decision 1 · sharing averagesK3 is not built. K4 is built as one state, “Tu ciudad todavía no abre”, with no count and no button; J5 has no second switch; P2 keeps 0.2.20's disclosure text. Nothing new leaves the phone until you decide.
  • IMSS · two detailsThe rule is the founder's and is built. M1–M4 take IVA off by multiplying by 0.84 and count each app on its own; dividing by 1.16 instead would make the amount $21,376, and adding the apps together would turn the two bars into one.
  • Decision 3 · the car as a weekly billBuilt as L4 and L5 draw it. No figure in the app includes the car.

Every figure comes from fixture.json, the v3 kit's own day of 19 Oct 2026 with a few inputs added, and check.py asserts each one inside its frame. Three things are examples and are marked as such in the fixture: the city averages in K3, the 37 of 200 drivers in K1, and the second driver in M2. The night theme is not drawn; the app follows the system as in v3.
