IMSS

The third tab answers one question: will I make it this month. The rule, as the founder states it: take what an app paid in the month (trips, incentives and referral incentives, without tips), take off the cost deduction (48 % for a vehicle today) and then IVA; if what is left reaches CDMX's monthly minimum wage, the driver qualifies for full coverage. For 2026 that means $21,938 paid by one app. The driver sees that one amount and a bar toward it, never the percentages. The three numbers behind it come from remote config, because they change.

¿Qué cambia si lo alcanzas?
  • Si una app te paga $21,938 o más en el mes, calificas para la cobertura completa del IMSS con esa app.
  • Cuentan tus viajes, tus incentivos y tus bonos por referidos. Las propinas no.
