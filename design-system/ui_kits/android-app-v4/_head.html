Thirty-eight screens

The whole app for launch: one chip, three tabs, and what sits behind the gear. It takes the place of the 108 screens in the v3 kit.

  • The chip is a colour and a numberKompara's K leads it, as a signature. A small ▲ ◆ ▼ repeats the colour for drivers who cannot tell red from green, and the unit says neto or bruto. Nothing on it repeats the verdict or the driver's own settings.
  • Three tabs, one per jobGanancias, Comparar, IMSS. The semáforo's health is one line at the top of Ganancias.
  • One meaning for every pesoWhat the trips paid, minus gasoline and wear. No figure includes the car, so none has to say whether it does.
  • The car is a weekly billOne bar on the week. First you cover it, then the rest is yours.
  • Estimates are explained onceIn Cómo se calculó, one tap from the number, and not on every label.
  • New since the reviewGanancias has two periods, Hoy and Semana. The month lives in the IMSS tab, the one place a driver needs it.
