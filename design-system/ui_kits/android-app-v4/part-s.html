The semáforo, over the ride app

One pill, 56 dp tall, 12 dp above the offer and flush with its left edge. Kompara's green K rides in an ink head, as on the app icon. It is the same on every offer and never sits on the verdict colour itself. Then colour, then the rate, with the word neto or bruto under the unit, whichever the driver chose. On a name-your-price offer the chip grows a tray with the counter-offers that would reach the driver's two limits. The ride apps are redrawn here without their brands; the second one stands for an app with a dark card and its own decline control.
