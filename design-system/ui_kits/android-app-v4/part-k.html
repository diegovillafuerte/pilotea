Comparar

Two parts, as in the original design. Tú compares the driver's own trips with each other: which app, which days, which hours. Tu ciudad says where the driver ranks among the city's drivers, as a percentile, overall and for the same three questions. The first part works from the first weeks, because the phone already stores each offer with its time. The second opens when enough drivers share their averages.
